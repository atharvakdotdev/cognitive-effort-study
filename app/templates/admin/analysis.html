Candidate subjective-value models

Varying task reward allows investigation of proportional scaling and interactions between reward, duration, and cognitive effort. These are candidate models only; this page does not fit them or identify a correct equation.

  1. M1 Additive: SV = R - aT - bE
  2. M2 Additive with interaction: SV = R - aT - bE - cTE
  3. M3 Multiplicative discounting: SV = R × exp[-(aT^q + bE^p + cT^qE^p)]
  4. M4 Hyperbolic discounting: SV = R / (1 + aT + bE + cTE)

Evaluate candidate models against trial-level choice data. N-back level is an operational task-demand measure rather than a perfect direct measure of cognitive effort; subjective ratings add a separate measure. The longer 246-choice session may increase fatigue and attrition and requires piloting for timing and quality effects.
