What to expect

Your choices matter

01

Practise

Remember letters using four N-back rules. Press Space for a match and F for no match, or tap the on-screen response buttons on a touch screen. Rate the mental demand after each practice block.

02

Choose

On each choice, compare the displayed credits without a task with the displayed task reward after a cognitive task. The task reward stays the same across five choices in that condition; the no-task reward may change.

03

Implement

After your choices, the computer randomly selects one non-control choice. If you chose the task on that choice, you will complete its exact task and duration.

How the N-back task works

Try free 0-back practice

There are real consequences. One of your 240 main choices is selected at random; control checks are never selected. Credits are experimental points with no monetary value and cannot be exchanged for money or other benefits.

For each letter, choose Match when it follows the rule and No match when it does not. You can tap the on-screen buttons or use Space for Match and F for No match. The rules are:

  • 0-back: match the letter X.
  • 1-back: match the letter shown one position earlier.
  • 2-back: match the letter shown two positions earlier.
  • 3-back: match the letter shown three positions earlier.

Each letter appears for 500 milliseconds, followed by a 2,000-millisecond blank interval. Keep the browser tab open.
