Debrief

About this study

The study asks how cognitive effort and task duration, separately and together, influence the subjective value of performing a task. N-back level is an operational measure of task demand, not a perfect measure of cognitive effort; your effort ratings provide an additional subjective measure.

The reward for a task was fixed at 10 credits. The data can compare candidate models at that reward level, but cannot estimate a complete reward-dependent value function from this experiment alone. The website records behaviour only and does not directly measure dopamine or brain activity.
