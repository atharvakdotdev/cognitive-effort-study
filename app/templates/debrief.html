Debrief

About this study

The study asks how reward, cognitive effort, and task duration, separately and together, influence the subjective value of performing a task. N-back level is an operational measure of task demand, not a perfect measure of cognitive effort; your effort ratings provide an additional subjective measure.

Task rewards varied between conditions and stayed fixed within each condition. The study compares candidate models but does not establish a correct equation. The website records behaviour only and does not directly measure dopamine or brain activity.
