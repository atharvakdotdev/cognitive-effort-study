Study information and consent

Before you begin

This study examines how the mental demand and duration of a cognitive task affect people's choices. It records behavioural choices and performance on short letter-memory tasks; it does not measure dopamine or brain activity.

You will answer a short comprehension check, practise four versions of an N-back task, rate their mental demand, make 86 reward choices, and complete one randomly selected choice if it requires a task. One choice will determine the task and credits recorded for your study outcome.

The session takes about 25–30 minutes. Taking part is voluntary. You may withdraw before final submission; using the withdraw control removes the responses collected in this session. The study does not request your name, email address, phone number, or IP address.

You may take part without an account. If you create a user account, choose a pseudonymous username: the account is linked to your participant code so you can resume your session. Passwords are stored as hashes. Your username is not included in research exports; survey responses use a random participant code and may be used for research analyses and shared in de-identified form. Credits are recorded by the study team for conversion under the study's payment arrangements; this site does not process payments.

Use the contact details provided with your study invitation if you have questions. This page does not claim that ethics approval has been obtained; the researcher must provide required approval and contact information before recruitment.
