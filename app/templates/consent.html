Study information and consent

Before you begin

Study procedure

This study looks at how the mental effort and duration of a task affect people's choices. It records your choices and performance on short letter-memory tasks. It does not measure dopamine or brain activity.

You will:

  • Answer a few questions to check your understanding.
  • Practise four versions of an N-back memory task.
  • Rate how mentally demanding each version feels.
  • Make 246 reward choices (240 main choices and 6 catch trials).

At the end, one of your 240 main choices will be randomly selected. You will either complete the selected task and receive its credits, or receive the alternative credits without doing the task.

About credits

Credits are just points used in this experiment. They have no monetary value and cannot be exchanged for money.

Participation and withdrawal

Taking part is completely voluntary. You can choose not to participate or withdraw before submitting your study. Your decision will not affect your friendship with the researcher.

Before submitting, you can use the Withdraw button to delete your session responses. After submitting, you can email the researcher within 30 days with your participant code to request deletion.

Your data

You can participate without an account. If you create one, use a username that does not reveal your identity. Your username is linked to a random participant code so you can resume your session. Passwords are stored securely as hashes.

Your name, phone number, and personal email address are not requested. Your IP address is not stored in the study database, although the website hosting provider may keep technical access logs.

Your responses will be used for research and may be shared without directly identifying information. Only the researcher, Atharva Dixit, can access the raw study data. Data will be deleted 30 days after your final submission.

Usernames are excluded from research exports. Study responses are pseudonymous because a participant code is linked to an account username.

Possible discomfort

The memory tasks may cause some mental fatigue or frustration. You can stop at any time before submitting.

Researcher

Atharva Dixit, independent researcher with no institutional affiliation. Contact: atharvak.dixit@gmail.com.

No ethics approval is claimed. The researcher must confirm applicable institutional and local ethics requirements before recruitment.
